How Fretwork works

One loop, four moves: describe the harmony, let the app find the scales, read why they work, then get them under your fingers.

  1. 1

    Build the progression

    2–12 chord cards, any root, 21 chord types. Click a symbol to hear it; ▶ plays the whole progression. The card you click becomes the focused chord.

    Studio → Progression
  2. 2

    Pick scales that fit

    Full fits grouped by mode family, near-fits with the clashing chord named, and chord-by-chord suggestions ranked by context. Chips light the neck — up to four scales overlay in color.

    Studio → Scales that fit
  3. 3

    Read the theory

    Why each scale fits, chord tones vs. tensions vs. avoid notes, target tones, voice-leading moves, arpeggio ideas, and a generated lick in tab — all following the focused chord.

    Studio → Harmony & theory
  4. 4

    Play it on the neck

    24 frets with degree-colored markers. Zoom from the full neck into CAGED positions, the seven 3-notes-per-string patterns, or click together a custom shape. Every note plays and explains itself.

    Studio → The neck

Theory library

Searchable scales, chords, and harmony topics. Progression entries have Send to Studio — they drop straight into step 1; scale entries have View on neck.

↻ The loop

Change a chord or scale and every panel recomputes — suggestions re-rank, theory rewrites, the neck repaints. There is no "done"; the loop is the practice session.

Focus follows you

The note map, arpeggios, lick, and the dashed rings on the fretboard all track whichever chord card you clicked last.

Open Fretwork Runs in any browser — audio synthesized live, nothing to install.
